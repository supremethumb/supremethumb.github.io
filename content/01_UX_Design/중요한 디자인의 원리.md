title: "중요한 디자인의 원리"

tags: ["UX", "디자인", "기획"]
description: "Helps direct the eye"
---

- Line
    - Helps direct the eye
    - Creates emphasis
    - Give a sense of movement
- Scale
    - Draw attention to and from certain elements
    - Creates emphasis / drama
- Colour
    - Create a strong palette
    - Use the right colour process
    - Consider colour theory
- Repetition
    - Helps to tie lots of individual elements together
    - Crucial for consistent branding
- Negative space
    - The space in between
    - Create clever images
    - Fantastic for logos
- Symmetry
    - Create a sens of calm
    - The human eye is generally attracted to symmetry
- Transparency
    - Helps element interaction
    - Can create movement
    - Use it intentionally
- Texture
    - Gives a tactility to designs
    - Gives depth to designs
    - Use it sparingly + intentionally
- Balance
    - Each element has a weight
    - Adjust your design's balance via scale and composition
- Hierarchy
    - Helps users navigate your design
    - Signals importance of elements
    - Use scale, line colours, etc.
- Contrast
    - Light vs Dark, Thick vs Thin
    - Helps create emphasis
    - Makes designs pop
- Framing
    - Helps highlight elements
    - Can be aesthetic or purposeful
    - Crop images in interesting ways
- Grid
    - Helps align elements
    - One grid can be used in many different ways
- Randomness
    - Don't be afraid to break rules
    - Asymmetry and cluuter can be effective visual tools
- Direction
    - Gives viewer a path
    - Direct the eye in 'Z' 'L' and 'Y' shapes
- Rules
    - Learn design rules but don't feel 100% limited to them
    - Break rules the right way
- Movement
    - Brings life to designs
    - Try blur effects, motion lines or waving effects
- Depth
    - Gives dimension to 2D designs
    - Try texture, shadows, 3D effects, horizon line, etc.
- Typography
    - Pick a distinct font palette that fits your design
    - Use wisely and carefully
- Composition
    - The arrangement of elements
    - Have purpose with composition
    - Use scale, depth and hierarchy
